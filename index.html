Sources & caveats

Read this before you cite it.

Source: Citizens Police Data Project (Invisible Institute), drawing on Chicago Police Department complaint records released to the public under Kalven v. City of Chicago (2014). Raw data: github.com/invinst/chicago-police-data. Burge and Guevara crew flagging compiled May 2026 from People's Law Office and Loevy & Loevy case records. Coverage is substantially complete only from 2000 forward; earlier records — including most of the Burge era — are missing.
  1. i. The named crew list is non-exhaustive — 24 confirmed officers (16 Burge, drawn from the Chicago Police Torture Archive; 8 Guevara, drawn from the Loevy & Loevy Guevara fact sheet). Other detectives named in individual TIRC referrals are not flagged here.
  2. ii. CPDP captures complaints to internal CPD investigative bodies. Federal civil suits, TIRC referrals, and Cook County Conviction Integrity Unit reviews are mostly invisible.
  3. iii. Pre-2000 records are missing or incomplete. For any officer who retired before ~2000, expect undercount.
  4. iv. One complaint can name multiple officers. Each officer-complaint pair is one row.
  5. v. “Disciplined” means some discipline was imposed — a 1-day suspension, a reprimand, or termination.
